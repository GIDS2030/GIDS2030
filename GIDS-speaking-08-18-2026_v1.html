The Talk

What John speaks about.

Why AI Initiatives Fail Before Anyone Writes a Line of Code

The RAND, MIT, and S&P Global data is consistent: 80–95% of enterprise AI initiatives fail to deliver promised value. The common explanation is data quality, talent gaps, or vendor selection. The actual cause is upstream — an organization that cannot describe its AI initiative in one sentence that a smart, non-technical colleague would push back on is not ready to build.

This talk gives boards, CIOs, and strategy teams the diagnostic frame to identify whether their current initiative is ready — and what to do before approving any further spend.

Grounded in the A.I.R. Framework™ and more than two decades of applied AI work across financial services, insurance, marketing, manufacturing, retail, and legal sectors.
