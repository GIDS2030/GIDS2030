The first conversation is a diagnosis, not a pitch.

What happens on the call

A 45-minute diagnostic session.

Not a sales call. John will ask you to describe your AI initiative in one sentence. From there, the conversation is a structured diagnosis.

1

You describe the initiative

One sentence. John listens for what is and isn't there.

2

GIDS identifies the upstream constraint

Is this a data readiness problem, a stakeholder alignment problem, a use-case clarity problem, an ROI definition problem, or a formulation problem?

3

You receive a clear next step

If GIDS is the right fit, John will tell you what a full engagement looks like. If not, he will tell you what is.

Right fit / Wrong fit

This assessment is not for everyone.

GIDS works with a small number of organizations at a time. The first call determines whether the fit is real.

Right fit

You have an AI initiative approved or under consideration and cannot yet state precisely what problem it solves

A board, CFO, or General Counsel will eventually ask whether this initiative was worth it

A prior AI initiative failed or stalled and you want to understand why before starting the next one

You are a CIO, CFO, or strategy lead at a company with real budget and real stakes

Wrong fit

You need someone to validate a decision already made

You are looking for a vendor to build or manage AI systems

You need a framework delivered as a presentation deck rather than an actionable diagnostic

You are at early-stage exploring AI generally with no specific initiative in progress
