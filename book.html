About the Book

A reasoning problem disguised as a technology problem.

The Augmented Intelligence Revolution is written for executives, board members, and strategists who need to ask better questions before they approve the next initiative. It is the practitioner's guide to the A.I.R. Framework™, grounded in more than a decade of applied machine-learning consulting across industries.

The argument

Failure happens before any model is selected. The upstream question is never asked. This book gives you the frame to ask it.

Who it's for

Executives, board members, and strategists who approve AI initiatives — and the advisors who brief them.

The framework

The A.I.R. Framework™ — Assess, Implement, Revolve — applied at book length, with real cases and diagnostic tools.

The standard

Can you describe your AI initiative in one sentence a smart, non-technical colleague would push back on? If not, you are not ready to build.

The Author

John Thomas Foxworthy

Most AI consultants started in software. John started in econometrics — where the cost of a wrong model is not a missed deadline. It's a number.

That discipline became a decade of applied machine-learning consulting, then three years teaching computer vision and deep learning, and ultimately an AI-governance and model-evaluation practice built for the organizations where it matters most.
